04 / COMMON QUESTIONS

Good to know.

Does the tracker require a subscription?

No. The tracker is offered as a one-time purchase. Future major upgrades may be sold separately.

What software do I need?

Microsoft Excel desktop is recommended. The workbook uses standard XLSX features and does not require macros.

How do I purchase the tracker?

Open the product details and select Buy Now. You will be redirected to our Payhip product page.

How will I receive my files?

After successful checkout, Payhip provides a download page and an email receipt with access to your purchased digital files.

Can I share or resell the workbook?

Redistribution and resale are not included in the proposed standard end-user licence. Please review the licence supplied with the product.

Can I request custom features?

Feature requests may be submitted through our Payhip store contact channel.
